**Tumor Necrosis Factor Alpha (TNF-α)** is a prototypical proinflammatory cytokine predominantly secreted by activated macrophages, particularly those polarized to the pro-inflammatory M1 phenotype, and serves as a central orchestrator of acute and chronic inflammatory diseases.¹⁰ TNF-α drives pathogenic inflammation through activation of key intracellular signaling cascades including NF-κB, JAK-STAT, and the [[nlrp3|NLRP3 inflammasome]], resulting in sustained inflammatory amplification, immune cell infiltration, and progressive tissue damage across diverse organ systems.²⁷ Its activity is tightly modulated by endogenous negative regulators including [[tnfaip3|A20 (TNFAIP3)]]⁶ and the NAD⁺-dependent deacetylase [[sirt1|SIRT1]],¹⁰ whose dysregulation exacerbates inflammatory pathology in aging and metabolic disease.

---

## Biology and regulation

TNF-α is predominantly produced by macrophages activated to the M1 phenotype in response to pathogen-associated molecular patterns, tissue injury, or metabolic stress.² In aged tissues, declining NAD⁺ availability impairs [[sirt1|SIRT1]] activity, leading to hyperacetylation of eukaryotic translation initiation factor 2α (eIF2α) and augmented endoplasmic reticulum stress in hepatic macrophages, which in turn drives exaggerated TNF-α secretion.¹⁰ The cytokine acts synergistically with [[il-6|interleukin-6 (IL-6)]] and [[il-1beta|IL-1β]] to amplify inflammatory cascades, establishing feed-forward loops that sustain tissue pathology.²⁹ Endogenous suppression is mediated by [[tnfaip3|A20]], a ubiquitin-editing enzyme that terminates NF-κB signaling; targeted activation of A20 via [[crispr-based-gene-editing-microspheres|CRISPRa-based gene-editing microspheres]] reduces TNF-α levels by over 30% in aged bone marrow stromal cells, rescuing osteogenic capacity.⁶

## Acute inflammatory diseases

In [[acute-pancreatitis|acute pancreatitis (AP)]], TNF-α is a primary driver of pancreatic tissue necrosis and systemic inflammatory response syndrome. The natural product derivative [[myrtucommulone-34|myrtucommulone-34 (compound 34)]], a potent soluble epoxide hydrolase (sEH) inhibitor, significantly protects against AP by elevating anti-inflammatory epoxyeicosatrienoic acids while suppressing TNF-α and IL-6 secretion.¹ Similarly, in acetaminophen-induced acute liver injury, therapeutic blockade of [[csf1r|colony-stimulating factor 1 receptor (CSF1R)]] with the selective inhibitor [[c52|C52]] during the early inflammatory phase markedly reduces circulating TNF-α and hepatic macrophage infiltration, accompanied by improved transaminase levels and histopathology.² In hepatic ischemia-reperfusion injury in aged mice, supplementation with [[nmn|β-nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN)]] restores [[sirt1|SIRT1]] activity, reduces eIF2α acetylation, and suppresses macrophage-derived TNF-α, iNOS, and IL-1β, mitigating late-phase injury exacerbation.¹⁰

## Autoimmune and chronic inflammatory diseases

In [[rheumatoid-arthritis|rheumatoid arthritis (RA)]], synovial infiltration by M1 macrophages secreting TNF-α, IL-1, and IL-6 activates JAK-STAT and NF-κB pathways, disrupting the osteoblast-osteoclast balance and driving cartilage degradation and bone erosion.³ A magnesium-doped mesoporous bioactive glass nanoplatform (MBGN-Tofa@PDA-FA) codelivering the JAK inhibitor tofacitinib and osteoimmunomodulatory ions suppresses TNF-α secretion by repolarizing macrophages from M1 to M2 phenotype, synergistically promoting osteoblast differentiation and bone regeneration.³ In ulcerative colitis, oral delivery of [[sitnf-alpha|anti-TNF-α siRNA]] via an inulin-derived hydrogel system ([[ig-sp-fk-lnps-sitnf-alpha|IG@SP@FK LNPs@siTNFα]]) enables colon-specific, cathepsin B/glutathione-responsive release, achieving efficient gene silencing and significant attenuation of disease progression.⁷

---

## Cardiovascular disease

In myocardial ischemia-reperfusion injury, TNF-α amplifies inflammatory damage through activation of the [[nlrp3|NLRP3 inflammasome]] and downstream cytokine cascades. Salvianolic acid B, a natural [[sirt2|SIRT2]] agonist delivered as cypate-functionalized organic quantum dots (SaB@qd@Cy), reduces infarct area by 41% and attenuates TNF-α and IL-1β expression via the SIRT2/NLRP3 pathway, demonstrating superior efficacy compared to mitochondrial-targeted antioxidants.⁸ The nanoplatform simultaneously enables near-infrared imaging of ischemic borders, integrating theranostic capabilities.

---

## Neurological and neurodevelopmental disorders

In cerebral palsy, sustained neuroinflammatory responses mediated by microglial activation drive progressive motor and cognitive impairment. Intravenously administered [[hucmsc-exos|human umbilical cord mesenchymal stem cell-derived exosomes (hUCMSC-Exos)]] significantly restore motor coordination and cognition in a rat model of moderate-to-severe cerebral palsy by attenuating ionized calcium-binding adapter molecule 1, TNF-α, and IL-6, while concurrently enhancing remyelination through promoted oligodendrocyte maturation.⁹ This dual modulation of neuroinflammation and repair processes establishes exosome therapy as a promising intervention for severe neurodevelopmental disorders.

---

## Metabolic and regenerative contexts

Age-related osteogenic impairment is driven by chronic inflammation-induced stem cell senescence, with elevated TNF-α suppressing osteoblast differentiation while activating osteoclasts. Injectable [[crispr-based-gene-editing-microspheres|CRISPRa-based microspheres]] delivering dCas9-VP64/sgRNA to activate [[tnfaip3|A20]] in bone marrow stromal cells reduce TNF-α by over 30%, increase osteogenic gene expression 4- to 5-fold, and achieve approximately 80% healing in aged critical-sized bone defects.⁶ In type 2 diabetic wound healing, a [[decellularized-dermal-scaffold|decellularized dermal scaffold]] combined with [[photobiomodulation-therapy|photobiomodulation therapy]] modulates TNF-α levels, accelerates wound contraction, and improves tensile strength by rebalancing the prolonged inflammatory microenvironment.⁵

---

## Therapeutic modalities

Direct TNF-α suppression via [[sirna|siRNA]] delivered through stimulus-responsive lipid nanoparticles represents a precision strategy for chronic inflammatory diseases, particularly when combined with microbiome-friendly polysaccharide hydrogels for oral colon-targeted delivery.⁷ Macrophage reprogramming from M1 to M2 phenotype constitutes an upstream therapeutic approach, achievable through CSF1R inhibition,² bioactive ion release from mesoporous nanocarriers,³ or metabolic rescue via [[nmn|NMN]]-mediated [[sirt1|SIRT1]] restoration.¹⁰ Epigenetic modulation through [[crispr-based-gene-editing-microspheres|CRISPRa activation]] of endogenous anti-inflammatory regulators like [[tnfaip3|A20]] offers a durable alternative to transient cytokine neutralization.⁶ In immunotherapy contexts, controlled TNF-α production by engineered CAR T cells enhances antitumor efficacy, with lipid nanoparticle-mediated in vivo CAR T engineering achieving 3.7-fold higher TNF-α secretion and 2.3-fold greater tumor growth inhibition.⁴
