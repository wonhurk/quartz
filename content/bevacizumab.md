**Bevacizumab** is a monoclonal antibody targeting [[vegfa|vascular endothelial growth factor A (VEGF-A)]] that inhibits tumor angiogenesis and promotes cancer cell death through dual mechanisms. Beyond its canonical anti-angiogenic function, bevacizumab induces apoptosis in [[glioblastoma]] (GBM) through a microRNA-dependent pathway that suppresses cell cycle regulators.¹ In [[clear-cell-renal-cell-carcinoma|clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC)]], bevacizumab suppresses [[flt1]]-driven epithelial-endothelial crosstalk, reducing cellular senescence and remodeling the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment.²

---

## Mechanism

Bevacizumab's antitumor activity operates through both established and recently elucidated pathways. As a VEGF-blocking monoclonal antibody, it inhibits endothelial growth factor signaling, thereby restricting tumor neovascularization and limiting oxygen and nutrient delivery. Beyond this canonical anti-angiogenic mechanism, mechanistic studies in GBM have revealed a distinct apoptotic branch. Bevacizumab treatment upregulates the microRNA [[mir-4695-5p]], which directly targets and inhibits expression of [[pkmyt1]], a membrane-associated tyrosine kinase critical for cell cycle progression and mitotic entry.¹ The suppression of [[pkmyt1]] rebalances pro- and anti-apoptotic factors, increasing [[bax]] expression while modulating Bcl-2 levels, collectively driving programmed cell death.¹

---

## Glioblastoma apoptosis

In [[glioblastoma|glioblastoma (GBM)]], bevacizumab elicits dose-dependent apoptotic responses through the [[mir-4695-5p]]/[[pkmyt1]] axis. When U87MG and U251 GBM cell lines are exposed to escalating bevacizumab concentrations, both apoptotic cell death and antiproliferative effects increase proportionally.¹ Functionally, the apoptotic phenotype is mechanistically dependent on [[mir-4695-5p]] upregulation: pharmacological inhibition of [[mir-4695-5p]] or overexpression of its downstream target [[pkmyt1]] substantially abrogates bevacizumab-induced cell death.¹ These rescue experiments establish [[mir-4695-5p]]-mediated suppression of [[pkmyt1]] as the critical nodal point in bevacizumab's pro-apoptotic mechanism, indicating that the microRNA-dependent circuit operates as a distinct pathway independent of the canonical anti-angiogenic signaling axis.

---

## Clear cell renal cell carcinoma

In [[clear-cell-renal-cell-carcinoma|ccRCC]], bevacizumab exerts antitumor effects through suppression of [[flt1]]-mediated epithelial-endothelial crosstalk. High [[flt1]] expression in endothelial cells, particularly when co-expressed with [[akt1]], drives malignant phenotypes through paracrine signaling to [[vegfa]]-expressing proximal tubular epithelial cells.² Bevacizumab treatment disrupts this autocrine-paracrine loop by antagonizing [[vegfa]] signaling, thereby suppressing [[flt1]]-driven cellular senescence in the tumor microenvironment.² The reduction of senescent cell burden removes a major source of immunosuppressive cytokines and chemokines, partially restoring [[cd8-t-cells|CD8+ T cell]] infiltration and antitumor immunity.² This mechanism reveals how VEGF inhibition extends beyond simple vascular regression to fundamentally reshape cellular senescence and immune activation in solid tumors.
