**Macrophages** are versatile phagocytic cells of the innate immune system that dynamically polarize between pro-inflammatory M1 and tissue-reparative M2 phenotypes in response to local microenvironmental cues. M1 macrophages secrete inflammatory cytokines including IL-1β, TNF-α, and IL-6, driving pathogen clearance and chronic inflammatory diseases, while M2 macrophages produce anti-inflammatory mediators such as TGF-β and IL-10 that promote tissue repair, angiogenesis, and extracellular matrix remodeling.¹ This phenotypic plasticity is reversible and therapeutically exploitable through engineered biomaterials, [[folic-acid|folic acid]]-targeted nanoparticles, and [[glucocorticoids|glucocorticoid]]-based immunomodulation.²³ Macrophage dysfunction underlies diverse pathologies spanning chronic inflammation, impaired wound healing, acute respiratory distress, and tumor progression, while their manipulation enables regenerative medicine and cancer immunotherapy strategies.

---

## Polarization mechanisms

Macrophage polarization is governed by distinct transcriptional programs activated by external stimuli. M1 polarization is triggered by interferon-γ and toll-like receptor ligands, activating the JAK-STAT and NF-κB pathways to upregulate inflammatory gene expression and enhance antimicrobial reactive oxygen species production.⁴ Conversely, M2 polarization induced by IL-4 or IL-13 activates STAT6 signaling, promoting expression of CD163, arginase-1, and secretion of pro-angiogenic growth factors.⁵ Lipid nanoparticles delivering IL-4 mRNA sustain M2 phenotype in inflammatory injury sites by maintaining autocrine IL-4 secretion, overriding endogenous pro-inflammatory signals.⁶ [[Glucocorticoids]] induce a distinct C1Q+ CD163+ macrophage subset with enhanced phagocytic capacity and IFN-γ-responsive CXCL9 secretion, distinct from classical M2 macrophages.⁷ Metabolic reprogramming accompanies phenotype shifts: M1 macrophages favor glycolysis and generate bactericidal nitric oxide, whereas M2 macrophages rely on oxidative phosphorylation and exhibit intercellular mitochondrial transfer to adjacent cells, amplifying tissue repair signals.¹

## Tissue regeneration

M2-polarized macrophages are central orchestrators of regenerative processes across multiple tissues. In critical-sized bone defects, pomegranate-inspired biomaterial composites featuring conductive microgels clear excessive reactive oxygen species while polarizing macrophages toward M2 phenotype, enabling osteogenic differentiation through intercellular mitochondrial transfer from macrophages to osteoblasts.¹ In [[volumetric-muscle-loss|volumetric muscle loss]], adoptively transferred macrophages pre-treated with IL4-loaded lipid nanoparticles maintain reparative phenotype for at least one week despite inflammatory microenvironments, promoting vascularization, muscle fiber regeneration, and scaffold remodeling while inducing M2 polarization in endogenous macrophage populations.⁶ 

Diabetic wound healing — severely impaired by neuropathy, vascular dysfunction, and chronic inflammation — is accelerated by cascade nanozyme hydrogel dressings that release nitric oxide and oxygen under near-infrared irradiation, simultaneously eradicating bacteria, polarizing macrophages to M2, and synergistically promoting angiogenesis and nerve regeneration.⁸ Single-cell RNA sequencing identified a CCL2-expressing adipose-derived stem cell subpopulation that coordinates wound repair through multipartite cellular networks: exosomes from these cells activate the PI3K/AKT/mTOR/HIF-1α axis in endothelial cells via CCL2-ACKR1 signaling, while engaging macrophages through IL34-CSF1R interactions to sustain M2 polarization.⁹ This CCL2-ADSC therapy significantly enhanced collagen deposition, vascularization, and wound closure in diabetic mouse models.⁹

## Inflammatory diseases

Aberrant macrophage activation drives tissue destruction in chronic inflammatory disorders. In [[rheumatoid-arthritis|rheumatoid arthritis]], synovial infiltration of M1-polarized macrophages secreting TNF-α, IL-1, and IL-6 activates osteoclastogenesis and disrupts the osteoblast-osteoclast balance, causing cartilage degradation and bone erosion.⁴ Magnesium-doped mesoporous bioactive glass nanoparticles co-delivering the JAK inhibitor tofacitinib and osteoimmunomodulatory Mg²⁺/Ca²⁺ ions achieve dual suppression of inflammation and promotion of osteoblast differentiation by repolarizing macrophages from M1 to M2 phenotype.⁴

In periodontitis, single-cell transcriptomic profiling identified IL1B+ macrophages as a pro-inflammatory subset closely linked to alveolar bone loss.¹⁰ These cells exhibit elevated [[serpinb9|SERPINB9]] (Spi6 in mice), a serine protease inhibitor that promotes macrophage survival and sustains inflammatory responses.¹⁰ Drug repurposing screens identified azilsartan medoxomil as a SERPINB9 inhibitor; its administration induced apoptosis of IL1B+ macrophages and attenuated experimental periodontitis.¹⁰ In age-related and inflammation-associated colonic dysfunction, dietary [[neu5ac|N-acetylneuraminic acid]] preserves intestinal homeostasis by suppressing macrophage infiltration and M1 polarization while maintaining tissue-reparative macrophages, mechanistically mediated through HIF-1α pathway suppression.¹¹

## Cardiac remodeling

Macrophage polarization critically regulates adverse cardiac remodeling following myocardial infarction. [[siglec-e|Siglec-E]], an immunoregulatory receptor upregulated on monocytes and macrophages post-MI, modulates macrophage function and inflammatory responses.¹² Siglec-E-deficient mice subjected to permanent coronary artery ligation exhibited worsened survival, impaired left ventricular ejection fraction, increased ventricular volumes, larger infarct size, and exacerbated fibrosis compared to wild-type controls.¹² Macrophage depletion and adoptive transfer studies confirmed that Siglec-E expression on macrophages is necessary and sufficient for cardioprotection, establishing it as a potential therapeutic target for preventing heart failure progression.¹² Targeting Siglec-E to shift macrophage polarization toward reparative phenotypes may offer novel strategies to attenuate post-infarction ventricular dysfunction.

## Host-pathogen interactions

Macrophages serve as primary phagocytic sentinels against bacterial pathogens, yet several microorganisms exploit macrophage biology to evade immune clearance. Klebsiella pneumoniae, an antimicrobial-resistant pathogen, employs iron-dependent mechanisms to survive within macrophages: microfluidic-proteomic profiling of non-phagocytosed bacteria revealed enrichment of [[iron-binding-proteins|iron-binding proteins]] and biosynthetic enzymes, and functional assays confirmed that iron supplementation promotes macrophage evasion and intracellular bacterial survival.¹³ Similarly, methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) establishes pneumonia through mechanisms countered by [[cu-mn-nps|Cu-Mn bimetallic nanoparticles]] that induce bacterial cuproptosis-like death, eliminate drug-resistant biofilms, and promote macrophage polarization toward bactericidal M1 phenotype while generating reactive oxygen species that disrupt bacterial membrane integrity.¹⁴ In vaccine-induced granulomas, macrophages containing [[gamma-aluminum-oxyhydroxide|γ-aluminum oxyhydroxide]] adjuvant undergo intracellular crystallization, forming crystalloid bodies through in vivo transformation of phagocytosed nanoparticles.¹⁵

## Cellular heterogeneity

Single-cell RNA sequencing has unveiled unprecedented macrophage heterogeneity beyond the M1/M2 paradigm. In adrenocortical carcinoma, [[glucocorticoids|glucocorticoid]] exposure polarizes macrophages toward a C1Q+ CD163+ subtype with enhanced phagocytic activity; upon IFN-γ stimulation, these cells secrete higher levels of the T cell chemoattractant CXCL9 than classical M1 macrophages.⁷ In severe [[pards|pediatric acute respiratory distress syndrome]], convergent immune abnormalities include distinct macrophage subsets exhibiting high interferon-stimulated gene expression but suppressed IL-1 pathway genes, associated with impaired leukocyte chemotaxis, phagocytosis, and M1 polarization — a phenotype recapitulated by IFN-γ priming that transcriptionally inhibits TLR7-induced IL-1β production.¹⁶ 

In [[axillary-lymph-node-metastasis|axillary lymph node metastasis]] from breast cancer, single-cell profiling revealed metastasis-specific expansion of monocytes, pre-B cells, and CD34+ hematopoietic stem cells, with complex ligand-receptor networks including [[ifi6|IFI6]] and other interferon-responsive genes mediating cross-talk between tumor epithelial cells and macrophages.¹⁷ CellChat reconstruction identified key communication axes involving TGFB1-TGFBR1 and IL34-CSF1R signaling that coordinate tumor-associated macrophage recruitment and polarization.¹⁷

## Therapeutic strategies

Multiple approaches harness macrophage biology for disease intervention. [[sahm-fa-tpp|Folic acid]]-modified nanoparticles achieve selective uptake by folate receptor-overexpressing macrophages: magnesium-doped bioactive glass nanoparticles co-delivering tofacitinib suppress JAK-STAT-driven inflammation while releasing osteoimmunomodulatory ions that promote M2 polarization and osteoblast differentiation in rheumatoid arthritis.⁴ Lipid nanoparticles delivering IL-4 mRNA enable macrophage cell therapy by inducing rapid transfection, IL-4 secretion, and sustained reparative phenotype modulation; pre-treated macrophages maintain M2 characteristics for at least one week post-transplantation, promoting muscle vascularization and regeneration in volumetric muscle loss.⁶

Bioactive nanoparticles leverage macrophage metabolism: [[cu-mn-nps|Cu-Mn bimetallic composites]] modified with PEGylated chitosan exhibit mucus-penetrating capabilities and lung-retention, generating reactive oxygen species that induce bacterial cuproptosis-like death while polarizing macrophages to bactericidal M1 phenotype.¹⁴ [[cascade-nanozyme-hydrogel-dressing|Cascade nanozyme hydrogels]] incorporating Cu/Mg-tannic acid nanozymes release nitric oxide and oxygen under near-infrared irradiation, simultaneously eradicating bacteria, modulating immune microenvironment toward M2 polarization, and promoting neuro-vascular coupling for diabetic wound repair.⁸ Natural compounds including [[liriopein-c|liriopein C]], a methylated homoisoflavonoid, exhibit dual direct cytotoxicity and immunomodulatory activity by enhancing macrophage-mediated tumor cell clearance in vivo.¹⁸
