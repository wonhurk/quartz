**Oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC)** is a highly aggressive malignancy of the oral cavity characterized by pronounced metabolic reprogramming and an immunosuppressive [[tumor-microenvironment|tumor microenvironment]] that confers resistance to conventional therapeutic approaches. Recent metabolomic profiling identifies a striking [[methionine]] metabolic abnormality strongly associated with poor patient prognosis.¹ OSCC exhibits elevated expression of the tumor-intrinsic survival protein [[cip2a|Cip2a (cancerous inhibitor of protein phosphatase 2A)]] and the immune evasion ligand [[pd-l1|PD-L1]], both of which limit the efficacy of [[immune-checkpoint-blockade|immune checkpoint blockade]]. Emerging therapeutic strategies integrate metabolic targeting via [[methionine]] deprivation with immunotherapy, combining engineered bacterial approaches and biomimetic nanoplatforms to simultaneously suppress tumor proliferation and overcome immunosuppression.

---

## Metabolic basis and methionine dependency

Bioinformatic analysis of clinical OSCC samples revealed a pronounced metabolic abnormality in [[methionine]] metabolism strongly correlated with poor patient prognosis.¹ In cultured OSCC cells, [[methionine]] deprivation drastically suppressed both cell proliferation and migration, establishing [[methionine]] as a critical metabolic dependency in this malignancy. Although dietary [[methionine]] restriction significantly inhibited tumor growth in OSCC-derived xenograft models, this approach induced severe systemic toxicity, precluding clinical translation. This limitation prompted development of tumor-selective [[methionine]] reduction strategies that preserve systemic [[methionine]] levels while achieving high local concentrations of [[methionine]]-targeting enzymes within malignant tissue.

---

## Engineered bacterial therapy

[[sgn1|SGN1]], a genetically attenuated strain of [[salmonella-typhimurium|Salmonella typhimurium]] engineered to overexpress L-methioninase (METase), was developed to selectively reduce [[methionine]] in tumor tissue while avoiding systemic toxicity.¹ Following intravenous delivery, SGN1 highly colonized the implanted tumors and specifically depleted [[methionine]] levels within malignant tissue, achieving superior anti-tumor efficacy in orthotopic OSCC models. A critical advantage of the [[sgn1|SGN1]] platform is its persistence within tumor tissue for at least seven days, enabling sustained [[methionine]] reduction even as the bacteria are rapidly cleared from non-tumor organs. Notably, [[sgn1|SGN1]] retained cytostatic activity in patient-derived organoids, underscoring clinical relevance. Comprehensive hematological, biochemical, and histopathological analyses confirmed a favorable safety profile, with no evidence of systemic toxicity attributable to the engineered bacterial strain.

## Immunosuppressive microenvironment and checkpoint resistance

OSCC exhibits elevated expression of both [[cip2a|Cip2a]] and [[pd-l1|PD-L1]], which together establish a dual mechanism of immune evasion: tumor-intrinsic survival signaling through [[cip2a|Cip2a]] suppression of protein phosphatase 2A and ligand-mediated suppression of [[pd-1|PD-1]]-expressing [[cd8-t-cells|CD8+ T cells]].² Analysis of clinical specimens and The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) data confirmed that elevated [[cip2a|Cip2a]] and [[pd-l1|PD-L1]] expression associate with poor prognosis. This dual immunosuppressive and tumor-intrinsic survival mechanism explains the limited efficacy of [[pd-1|PD-1]] checkpoint blockade monotherapy in OSCC, motivating combination strategies that simultaneously target tumor-intrinsic survival pathways and immune evasion mechanisms.

---

## Biomimetic nanoplatform combining metabolic and immunological targeting

[[si-cip2a-pml|siCip2a@PML]] is a biomimetic nanoplatform integrating siRNA-mediated [[cip2a|Cip2a]] suppression with competitive [[pd-1|PD-1]]/[[pd-l1|PD-L1]] checkpoint blockade.² The platform comprises lipid nanoparticles encapsulating [[cip2a]] siRNA, which induces [[apoptosis]] through [[cip2a|Cip2a]] silencing, and is cloaked with a [[pd-1]]-overexpressing macrophage membrane to enable dual targeting and immune engagement. In vitro and in vivo studies demonstrated enhanced tumor accumulation, efficient [[cip2a|Cip2a]] knockdown, and potent anti-tumor activity. Critically, the [[pd-1]]-functionalized membrane camouflage promoted [[dendritic-cells|dendritic cell]] maturation, increased intratumoral infiltration of [[cd4-t-cells|CD4+ T cells]] and [[cd8-t-cells|CD8+ T cells]], and restored anti-tumor immune responses. By uniting tumor-intrinsic growth suppression via [[cip2a|Cip2a]] silencing with [[pd-1|PD-1]]-mediated immune checkpoint modulation in a single delivery system, this combinatorial approach addresses the dual pathogenic mechanisms limiting conventional monotherapy efficacy in OSCC.
