**Gastric cancer** is a malignant neoplasm arising from the stomach lining or gastroesophageal junction, representing the fifth most diagnosed cancer worldwide and the fifth leading cause of cancer-related mortality. The disease is characterized by aggressive metastatic potential — particularly peritoneal dissemination — and carries a dismal prognosis, with median overall survival under one year for metastatic disease in the absence of targeted intervention.¹ Environmental exposures including [[helicobacter-pylori|Helicobacter pylori]] infection and dietary [[nitrites|nitrite]] compounds are established initiating factors.² Therapeutic advances have recently transformed outcomes for HER2-positive tumors, with [[trastuzumab]]-based immunotherapy achieving median survival exceeding 20 months,³ while emerging molecular targets such as [[cldn18.2|CLDN18.2]] and diagnostic tools including [[mir-106a|miRNA-106a]] biosensors and [[inception-resnetv2|AI-powered imaging models]] are reshaping clinical management.

---

## Disease burden and epidemiology

Gastric cancer remains a predominant public health challenge in East Asia, where incidence rates are highest globally.⁴ The majority of cancer-related deaths in gastric cancer arise from metastatic tumors that disseminate from malignant primary sites, with peritoneal metastasis representing a particularly lethal pattern that limits surgical options and reduces survival.⁵ The low sensitivity of conventional diagnostic methods for detecting occult peritoneal spread at presentation has historically contributed to poor outcomes, as patients with advanced T3/T4 stage disease frequently harbor microscopic dissemination undetectable by standard imaging.⁵

## Molecular mechanisms of progression

Multiple converging pathways drive gastric cancer metastasis and immune evasion. Under hypoxic conditions, hypoxia-inducible factor 2-alpha (HIF-2α) transcriptionally activates the integrin ITGβ5 gene, leading to secretion of ITGβ5-enriched exosomes that mediate intercellular communication and promote tumor cell migration and invasion.¹ Simultaneously, tumor-derived serglycin (SRGN) binds to CD44 on T cells, driving differentiation of a specific subset of LAG3-positive regulatory T cells (Tregs) through activation of the CD44-TGFβRI-SMAD3 signaling axis.⁶ This SRGN-CD44 interaction triggers glycolysis and facilitates reactive oxygen species clearance in Tregs, attenuating oxidative modification at the LAG3 methionine 171 residue and stabilizing LAG3 protein through reduced K48-linked ubiquitination, thereby maintaining immunosuppressive function within the tumor microenvironment.⁶

Exosome-mediated transfer of oncogenic circular RNAs represents an additional mechanism of malignant transformation. [[circ0000549|Circ0000549]], markedly upregulated in cells exposed to the nitrosating agent N-methyl-N'-nitro-N-nitrosoguanidine (MNNG) and in serum exosomes from patients with high nitrite exposure history, functions as a competing endogenous RNA that sequesters miR-15b-5p, thereby derepressing its target KIF1B and enhancing epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) and stemness properties.⁷ Similarly, [[kirrel1|KIRREL1]] — a cell surface protein significantly upregulated in gastric cancer tissues — positively regulates the EMT markers vimentin, MMP2, and MMP9, promoting proliferation, invasion, and chemoresistance to 5-fluorouracil while correlating with poor progression-free and overall survival.⁸

## Environmental risk factors

[[helicobacter-pylori|Helicobacter pylori]] infection is the predominant environmental driver of gastric cancer initiation, and treatment of this bacterium reduces gastric cancer incidence and mortality across all age groups.² Dietary exposure to [[nitrites|nitrites]] — which can be metabolically converted to N-nitroso compounds such as MNNG — plays a critical complementary role in carcinogenesis, particularly in regions with high nitrite consumption.⁷ The malignant transformation induced by MNNG exposure is propagated through exosomal signaling, whereby transformed cells secrete exosomes enriched in oncogenic circRNAs that influence recipient normal cells and promote progression along the cancer continuum.⁷

## Therapeutic advances

The most significant recent breakthrough in gastric cancer therapy came with FDA accelerated approval in 2021 and regular approval in 2025 of pembrolizumab combined with [[trastuzumab]] and platinum-based chemotherapy for unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive gastric or gastroesophageal junction carcinoma.³ The pivotal KEYNOTE-811 trial demonstrated an overall response rate of 74.4% compared to 51.9% with placebo, and a median overall survival of 20.0 months versus 16.8 months.³ Exploratory analyses revealed that treatment benefit was predominantly driven by patients with programmed death ligand 1 (PD-L1) combined positive score ≥1, who comprised 85% of the cohort and showed a hazard ratio of 0.79, whereas the PD-L1-negative subgroup did not demonstrate survival improvement.³

[[cldn18.2|CLDN18.2]] has emerged as a promising therapeutic target in gastric cancers, although its clinical efficacy in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma has been modest.⁹ Recent work has identified that KRAS mutation-driven O-GlcNAcylation of CLDN18.2 reduces the efficacy of CLDN18.2-targeted therapy, suggesting that post-translational modifications modulate target accessibility and therapeutic response.⁹ Separately, [[hsp90-targeted-radiotracer|HSP90-targeted radiotracers]] have been developed and clinically validated to enable tumor delineation and patient stratification for emerging HSP90 inhibitors, achieving high tumor uptake in colorectal and gastric cancer models while minimizing off-target accumulation in liver and kidneys.¹⁰

## Diagnostic innovations

Early detection remains critical for improving survival in gastric cancer. [[mir-106a|MiRNA-106a]], abnormally overexpressed in gastric cancer tissues, has been harnessed as a liquid biopsy biomarker using an ultrasensitive [[fg-cntfet|floating-gate carbon nanotube field-effect transistor (FG-CNTFET)]] biosensor functionalized with tetrahedral DNA nanostructure probes.¹¹ This platform achieves a detection limit as low as 7 attomolar in static mode and 1 picomolar under dynamic conditions, with preliminary clinical evaluation demonstrating capacity to distinguish patients from healthy donors.¹¹ The floating-gate layer physically isolates the nanotube channel from the biological environment while amplifying surface potential changes via capacitive coupling, thereby enhancing both stability and sensitivity.¹¹

For patients with locally advanced T3/T4 stage gastric cancer, artificial intelligence-based analysis of contrast-enhanced computed tomography images has shown remarkable performance in predicting occult peritoneal metastasis.⁵ An [[inception-resnetv2|Inception-ResNetV2]] model augmented with squeeze-and-excitation attention mechanisms achieved a receiver operating characteristic area under the curve of 0.973, Precision-Recall AUC of 0.908, and F1-score of 0.818, outperforming conventional convolutional neural networks.⁵ This approach enables risk stratification to guide staging laparoscopy decisions and minimize unnecessary surgery.⁵
