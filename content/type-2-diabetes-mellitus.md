**Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM)** is a chronic metabolic disease characterized by persistent hyperglycemia resulting from impaired insulin secretion and insulin resistance, affecting multiple organ systems and representing a major global health burden.¹⁰ The pathogenesis involves dysregulation of hepatic signaling pathways—particularly the IRS2/PI3K-Akt/FoxO1 axis—alongside abnormal aggregation of [[hiapp|human islet amyloid polypeptide (hIAPP)]], alterations in gut microbiota composition, and profound disturbances in lipid and glycerophospholipid metabolism.¹² T2DM manifests in diverse complications including [[diabetic-cardiomyopathy|diabetic cardiomyopathy]] with coupled dysregulation of mitophagy and extracellular matrix remodeling, impaired wound healing due to insufficient angiogenesis and prolonged inflammation, and increased risk of [[myocardial-infarction|myocardial infarction]] and cardiovascular events.⁴⁵⁸

---

## Pathogenesis

The molecular pathogenesis of T2DM is multifactorial, with [[hiapp|hIAPP]] aggregation emerging as a critical contributor to pancreatic β-cell dysfunction and disease progression.³ These β-sheet-rich amyloid aggregates accumulate in pancreatic islets and are targeted by peptide-based disruption strategies that exploit aspartimide formation to generate [[aspartyl-peptides|anti-amyloidogenic peptidomimetics]].³ In the liver, therapeutic compounds such as [[heterophyllin-b|heterophyllin B]] restore metabolic homeostasis by modulating the hepatic IRS2/PI3K-Akt/FoxO1 signaling pathway, thereby correcting aberrant bile acid and glycerophospholipid metabolism that characterize the diabetic state.¹ Gut microbiota dysbiosis represents another core pathogenic mechanism, with acetylation-modified konjac glucomannan enriching beneficial Bacteroides species—particularly Bacteroides uniformis and Bacteroides coprocola—in a dose-dependent manner that correlates with acetylation degree and subsequently improves glucose metabolism, lipid profiles, and systemic inflammation.²

## Cardiovascular and wound healing complications

[[Diabetic-cardiomyopathy|Diabetic cardiomyopathy]] in T2DM is characterized by coupled dysregulation across lipid metabolism, mitochondrial quality control through mitophagy, and extracellular matrix remodeling pathways.⁵ Multi-omics profiling of diabetic human myocardium reveals a complex molecular landscape in which these three processes interact to drive cardiac dysfunction and structural remodeling. Abnormal myocardial perfusion reserve—a surrogate marker for [[myocardial-ischemia|myocardial ischemia]]—and prior [[myocardial-infarction|myocardial infarction]] offer incremental prognostic value in predicting major adverse cardiovascular and cerebrovascular events in patients with T2DM.⁸ Individuals harboring both abnormal perfusion reserve and a history of infarction face substantially elevated risk, establishing these parameters as critical clinical stratification tools.

Impaired wound healing in T2DM results from dysregulated angiogenic responses, defective extracellular matrix remodeling, insufficient revascularization, and a chronically prolonged inflammatory microenvironment.⁴ Combined intervention with [[decellularized-dermal-scaffold|decellularized dermal scaffolds]] and [[photobiomodulation-therapy|photobiomodulation therapy]] as an adjunct biophysical stimulus markedly accelerates wound contraction, enhances tensile strength, and improves structural tissue organization by modulating cellular angiogenic, inflammatory, and matrix remodeling responses at both the cellular and tissue levels.⁴

## Therapeutic strategies

Glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonists such as [[liraglutide|liraglutide]] represent a cornerstone of contemporary T2DM management, offering robust glycemic control through enhancement of insulin secretion and suppression of glucagon release.⁶⁷ Novel oral delivery systems utilizing virus-inspired nanocarriers—comprising hollow mesoporous silica nanoparticles functionalized with cell-penetrating peptides and hyaluronic acid—achieve high encapsulation efficiency and pH-responsive release profiles that protect peptide therapeutics from gastric degradation while enhancing intestinal absorption.⁶ Despite their efficacy, GLP-1 receptor agonists require vigilant monitoring for rare adverse events including nonarteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy, a vision-threatening complication whose incidence appears elevated compared to dipeptidyl peptidase 4 inhibitor use.⁷

[[Sglt2-inhibitors|Sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitors]] provide renal and cardiovascular benefits in T2DM management but exert limited direct effects on appetite regulation and body weight despite their therapeutic utility in promoting glycosuria and reducing hyperglycemia.¹¹ [[Automated-insulin-delivery|Automated insulin delivery (AID)]] systems employing simplified meal bolus strategies demonstrate safety and effectiveness in adults with insulin-treated T2DM, offering improved glycemic outcomes through real-time glucose-responsive insulin modulation.⁶

## Prediabetes intervention window

[[Prediabetes|Prediabetes]]—characterized by elevated blood glucose levels that do not meet diagnostic criteria for T2DM—represents a critical intervention window in which early dietary and metabolic interventions can prevent disease progression.² Acetylation-modified konjac glucomannan administered during this period enriches beneficial gut microbiota, upregulates protective metabolites such as D-pantothenic acid, and ameliorates glucose dysregulation, dyslipidemia, inflammation, and hepatic steatosis in a structure-dependent manner positively correlated with acetylation degree.² Risk stratification tools such as the [[findrisc|revised FINDRISC]] incorporate sociodemographic indicators to improve identification of individuals at high risk for T2DM across diverse populations, facilitating targeted early intervention strategies.¹⁰

---

## Novel therapeutic agents

[[Heterophyllin-b|Heterophyllin B]], a cyclic peptide derived from Pseudostellaria Radix, alleviates T2DM by modulating the hepatic IRS2/PI3K-Akt/FoxO1 signaling pathway and restoring bile acid and glycerophospholipid homeostasis.¹ An engineered oral delivery system employing N-acetylgalactosamine-modified pillar[6]arene for liver targeting, encapsulated within a mucoadhesive interpolymer complex micropatch, achieves oral bioavailability of 66.31%—approximately 3.3-fold higher than free heterophyllin B—through reversible tight junction modulation, P-glycoprotein efflux inhibition, and activation of clathrin- and caveolae-mediated endocytosis.¹ In high-fat diet/streptozotocin-induced T2DM models, this formulation outperforms [[metformin]] in improving glycemic control, insulin sensitivity, and lipid metabolism.¹

Smart [[aspartyl-peptides|post-transmuting anti-amyloidogenic peptidomimetics]] operate through a pro-drug mechanism, infiltrating aggregating [[hiapp|hIAPP]] systems as standard peptides before undergoing aspartimide-induced chemical transformations that generate structural kinks, misalign β-sheet topology, and significantly disrupt amyloid aggregates through racemization and nucleophilic ring opening.³
