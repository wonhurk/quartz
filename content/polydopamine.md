**Polydopamine (PDA)** is a synthetic, biocompatible polymer inspired by the adhesive proteins of marine mussels, characterized by intrinsic metal ion-chelating properties, antioxidant activity, and the ability to form conformal coatings on virtually any substrate. Its functional versatility has established PDA as a foundational platform in nanomedicine, where it operates simultaneously as a protective barrier, a multi-agent cargo carrier, and an active therapeutic component. The material's capacity to scavenge excessive calcium and iron ions addresses pathological metal dysregulation in inflammatory microenvironments, while its mesoporous formulations enable sustained co-delivery of drugs, nanoparticles, exosomes, and bioactive peptides across diverse therapeutic applications.¹⁻¹⁰

---

## Material properties

Polydopamine is synthesized through oxidative polymerization of dopamine under alkaline conditions, forming a melanin-like structure rich in catechol, amine, and imine functional groups. These groups confer strong metal-binding affinity, enabling PDA to chelate Ca²⁺ and Fe²⁺ ions that accumulate pathologically in inflammatory tissues.² By sequestering excess [[calcium-dysregulation|calcium]], PDA disrupts endoplasmic reticulum-mitochondrial Ca²⁺ crosstalk that drives macrophage proinflammatory activation, while iron chelation stabilizes cellular iron homeostasis and suppresses ferroptosis through GSH-GPX4 axis modulation.¹ Mesoporous polydopamine (MPDA), synthesized via soft-templating methods, provides exceptionally high surface area that accommodates simultaneous loading of [[curcumin]], [[silver-nanoparticles|silver nanoparticles]], and [[bone-morphogenetic-protein-9|BMP9 peptides]], with sustained release kinetics tunable by pore architecture.²˒⁹˒¹⁰ PDA coatings exhibit intrinsic photothermal conversion efficiency under near-infrared irradiation, generating localized hyperthermia that synergizes with loaded therapeutics for combined antibacterial and antitumor effects.⁴˒⁹˒¹⁰

## Oral drug delivery

PDA coating has emerged as a critical enabler of oral biologics delivery by conferring gastric acid resistance and enabling site-specific release in the gastrointestinal tract. In ulcerative colitis therapy, PDA-coated probiotic systems (LEG@PDA) protect Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG and [[curcumin|emodin]]-loaded lactoferrin nanoparticles during gastric transit, then release the payload at colonic sites where lactoferrin-mediated epithelial targeting and M cell transcytosis facilitate mucus layer restoration through the CGRP-RAMP1 axis.¹ Similarly, PDA-coated microbial platforms—engineered *Escherichia coli* BL21 and *Pichia pastoris* X33—deliver CRISPR/Cas9 systems targeting ART1 in colorectal cancer, with PDA ensuring microbial viability and intestinal retention to establish sustained gene-editing factories *in situ*.⁵ This protective function extends to [[extracellular-vesicles|exosome]] delivery, where PDA stabilizes vesicle integrity against enzymatic degradation while permitting controlled release triggered by local pH or enzymatic cues.³

## Tissue engineering and regenerative medicine

In periodontal and bone regeneration, PDA serves dual functions as an immunomodulatory agent and as a scaffold-integrating adhesive. The [[osteoimmunomodulatory-microneedle|osteoimmunomodulatory microneedle]] platform (OIMN) combines PDA nanoparticles loaded with [[bone-morphogenetic-protein-9|BMP9 peptide]] and integrated into decellularized extracellular matrix to overcome anatomical delivery barriers in periodontal defects.² By chelating excess Ca²⁺, PDA-BMP9 nanoparticles (PDAB NPs) restore endoplasmic reticulum and mitochondrial homeostasis, polarizing macrophages from M1 to M2 phenotype and accelerating osteoblast differentiation of bone mesenchymal stem cells.² Piezoelectric fibrous membranes coated with PDA enable sustained release of periodontal ligament-derived [[extracellular-vesicles|exosomes]], with the combined electroactive stimulation and bioactive cargo synergistically promoting vascularized periodontal bone regeneration in rat defect models.³ For infected wound management, functionally graded bilayer polyurethane sponges incorporate a PDA-coated upper layer bearing [[silver-nanoparticles|silver nanoparticles]] (PUF@P-Ag), providing stage-adaptive functions: hemostasis via microporous hydrophilic base, triple-mode antibacterial action (acidic pH, silver ion release, photothermal effect), and oxidative stress relief through PDA's intrinsic antioxidant activity.⁴

## Multifunctional nanotheranostics

PDA's capacity for multi-agent loading and [[photoacoustic-imaging|photoacoustic imaging]] has been exploited in precision medicine platforms targeting rheumatoid arthritis and bacterial keratitis. In rheumatoid arthritis, magnesium-doped mesoporous bioactive glass nanoparticles coated with PDA and functionalized with folic acid (MBGN-Tofa@PDA-FA) co-deliver tofacitinib and osteoimmunomodulatory Mg²⁺/Ca²⁺ ions to folate receptor-overexpressing macrophages, suppressing JAK-STAT/NF-κB signaling while promoting M1-to-M2 repolarization and osteoblast differentiation.⁶ PDA coating enables real-time [[photoacoustic-imaging|photoacoustic imaging]] of nanoparticle biodistribution, coupling diagnostic monitoring with therapeutic intervention.⁶ For bacterial keratitis, mesoporous PDA platforms (Q/AMP) simultaneously load [[silver-nanoparticles|silver nanoparticles]] and quercetin, achieving broad-spectrum antibacterial efficacy (97.0% versus 77.4% for levofloxacin) while inhibiting corneal neovascularization through suppression of human umbilical vein endothelial cell proliferation and tube formation.⁹ In acne vulgaris treatment, [[curcumin]]-loaded MPDA embedded in hyaluronic acid/fucoidan microneedles (Cur-MPDA@HA/FPS MNs) combines transdermal delivery with photothermal activation, achieving 99.4% antibacterial efficacy against *Propionibacterium acnes* under NIR irradiation while scavenging reactive oxygen species to reduce inflammation.¹⁰

## Diagnostic and monitoring applications

PDA's electrochemical properties and biocompatibility enable integration into real-time biosensing platforms. The [[neurosense|NeuroSense]] system incorporates PDA-based pH sensors alongside glucose and lactate aptamer biosensors within external ventricular drainage systems, providing near real-time monitoring of cerebrospinal fluid biomarkers critical for detecting infection and drain malfunction in intensive care settings.⁸ PDA's compatibility with ethylene-oxide sterilization and stable sensor performance in human CSF for multiple days positions it for clinical translation in neurocritical care.⁸ In point-of-care diagnostics, hierarchically mesoporous PDA scaffolds confine platinum nanozymes within magnetic core-shell dendritic silica (FeMPPt), serving as enrichment-confinement nanoreactors that amplify plasmonic gold signals in lateral flow immunoassays by 125-fold compared to conventional gold nanoparticles, enabling ultrasensitive detection of cardiac troponin I and alpha-fetoprotein with 125/230-fold and 1300/1300-fold improvements in qualitative/quantitative limits of detection, respectively.⁷
