**Interleukin-1β (IL-1β)** is a potent pro-inflammatory cytokine produced primarily by macrophages and immune cells through [[inflammasome|NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation, serving as a central mediator of pathogenic inflammation across acute and chronic disease states.¹⁻³ IL1B-expressing macrophages constitute a distinct pro-inflammatory subset whose expansion correlates with tissue destruction and functional impairment in periodontitis and other inflammatory conditions.³ The cytokine is subject to multi-axis regulatory control involving the [[ahr|aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR)]], [[sirt2|SIRT2]], and [[sirt1|SIRT1]]-dependent pathways, making it a convergent therapeutic target across neurological, cardiovascular, and infectious diseases.²⁴⁵

---

## Production and cellular source

IL-1β is generated through proteolytic cleavage of pro-IL-1β by caspase-1 following [[inflammasome|NLRP3 inflammasome]] assembly in response to pathogen-associated molecular patterns and damage-associated molecular patterns.¹⁹ Single-cell transcriptomic profiling has identified IL1B⁺ macrophages as a transcriptionally distinct pro-inflammatory subset characterized by elevated expression of tissue-degrading enzymes and sustained inflammatory signaling.³ In the central nervous system, both resident microglia and infiltrating macrophages produce IL-1β following ischemic injury, while astrocytes contribute to cytokine amplification through NF-κB-dependent transcription.² [[zinc-finger-protein-148|ZFP148]] directly binds the Il1b promoter to activate transcription during [[inflammasome|PANoptosis]], linking chromatin accessibility to inflammatory gene expression.⁶ The cytokine is also induced pharmacologically by [[tlr7|TLR7]] agonists, though this response is suppressed when cells are pre-exposed to interferon-γ through transcriptional inhibition of downstream inflammatory pathways.⁷

---

## Ischemic and traumatic injury

IL-1β drives secondary neurodegeneration in ischemic stroke and spinal cord injury through multiple cytotoxic mechanisms. In experimental stroke models, [[nadph|NADPH]] treatment suppresses [[inflammasome|NLRP3 inflammasome]] activation and reduces IL-1β expression by approximately 16%, coinciding with reduced infarct volume (∼50%), attenuated cerebral edema, and improved neurological function.¹ [[indole-3-propionic-acid|Indole-3-propionic acid (IPA)]], a tryptophan-derived gut microbiota metabolite, attenuates IL-1β expression in astrocytes following spinal cord injury by activating the [[ahr|AhR]] receptor, which suppresses NF-κB and MAPK signaling pathways.² This intervention reduces glial scar formation, enhances neuronal survival, and improves long-term motor function as measured by BMS scores and gait coordination.² In myocardial ischemia-reperfusion injury, salvianolic acid B delivered via organic quantum dots functions as a [[sirt2|SIRT2]] agonist, reducing IL-1β and TNF-α expression through inhibition of the [[inflammasome|NLRP3 inflammasome]] and decreasing infarct area by 41%.⁹

## Periodontitis and tissue destruction

In periodontitis, IL1B⁺ macrophages form a pro-inflammatory subset that drives progressive alveolar bone loss and periodontal tissue destruction.³ These cells exhibit elevated expression of SERPINB9, a serine protease inhibitor that promotes macrophage survival and sustains chronic inflammatory responses.³ Machine-learning analysis of single-cell RNA sequencing data identified SERPINB9 as a critical regulator of IL1B⁺ macrophage persistence, and pharmacological inhibition with repurposed Azilsartan medoxomil attenuates inflammatory macrophage responses and alleviates alveolar bone loss in experimental models.³ This finding establishes the IL1B⁺ macrophage subset as a cell-intrinsic driver of tissue destruction and a viable therapeutic target in chronic inflammatory diseases.

## Pediatric acute respiratory distress syndrome

Severe [[pards|pediatric acute respiratory distress syndrome (PARDS)]] is characterized by a paradoxical suppression of IL-1 pathway genes in pulmonary macrophages despite strong interferon-stimulated gene expression.⁷ Reduced pulmonary IL-1α and IL-1β levels are associated with impaired leukocyte chemotaxis, phagocytosis, and M1 polarization, while IFN-γ remains elevated.⁷ In vitro modeling demonstrates that IFN-γ priming suppresses [[tlr7|TLR7]]-induced IL-1β production through transcriptional inhibition of downstream inflammatory pathways, recapitulating the immune signature observed in patient samples.⁷ This interferon-driven IL-1 suppression distinguishes [[pards|PARDS]] from other inflammatory lung diseases and highlights compartmentalized immune dysregulation as a central pathogenic feature.

---

## Regulatory suppression pathways

IL-1β expression is controlled through multiple convergent regulatory axes that modulate [[inflammasome]] activation, chromatin accessibility, and post-translational signaling. The [[ahr|aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR)]] pathway suppresses IL-1β in astrocytes via [[indole-3-propionic-acid|indole-3-propionic acid]]-mediated inhibition of NF-κB and MAPK phosphorylation.² [[sirt2|SIRT2]] agonism by salvianolic acid B reduces IL-1β expression in cardiac tissue by inactivating the [[inflammasome|NLRP3 inflammasome]], preventing caspase-1 cleavage of pro-IL-1β.⁹ In hepatic macrophages, NAD⁺ depletion during ischemia-reperfusion injury reduces [[sirt1|SIRT1]] deacetylase activity, leading to eIF2α hyperacetylation and pro-inflammatory polarization with elevated IL-1β.¹⁰ Supplementation with [[nmn|β-nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN)]] restores [[sirt1|SIRT1]] activity, reduces eIF2α acetylation, alleviates endoplasmic reticulum stress, and suppresses IL-1β expression.¹⁰ [[selenium-nanoparticles|Selenium nanoparticles]] attenuate IL-1β mRNA levels in Klebsiella pneumoniae-induced mastitis by restoring glutathione activity and suppressing ferroptosis.¹¹

---

## Downstream signaling and miRNA regulation

IL-1β directly regulates microRNA-132 (miR-132) expression, establishing a link between inflammatory signaling and apoptotic pathways in [[calcific-aortic-valve-disease|calcific aortic valve disease (CAVD)]].¹² Proteomic and transcriptomic analyses revealed dysregulation of the bcl2/bax apoptosis axis in CAVD, with IL-1β-mediated miR-132 suppression contributing to increased cellular apoptosis in valve interstitial cells.¹² [[bcl2-engineered-exosomes|Exosomes derived from Bcl-2-engineered induced pluripotent stem cell cardiomyocytes]] restore miR-132 expression, upregulate bcl2, downregulate bax, and inhibit abnormal elevation of inflammatory markers including IL-1β.¹² This regulatory circuit demonstrates how IL-1β coordinates inflammatory and apoptotic programs in cardiovascular pathology.

---

## Therapeutic modulation and outcomes

Pharmacological suppression of IL-1β improves functional outcomes across multiple disease models. In ischemic stroke, [[nadph|NADPH]]-mediated IL-1β reduction preserves blood-brain barrier integrity by upregulating tight junction proteins (ZO-1 expression increased ∼29%) and reducing matrix metalloproteinase-9 levels by ∼23%.¹ In diabetic wounds treated with [[decellularized-dermal-scaffold|decellularized dermal scaffold]] combined with [[photobiomodulation-therapy|photobiomodulation therapy]], reduced IL-1β expression coincides with accelerated wound contraction, improved tensile strength, enhanced angiogenesis, and increased collagen deposition.⁸ [[selenium-nanoparticles|Selenium nanoparticle]] administration in bacterial mastitis reduces histopathological damage, neutrophil infiltration, and IL-1β mRNA expression while restoring anti-inflammatory IL-10 levels and improving mammary hemodynamics.¹¹ In ulcerative colitis, colon-targeted siRNA nanoparticles encapsulated in [[ig-sp-fk-lnps-sitnf-alpha|inulin-derived hydrogel]] systems attenuate IL-1β expression indirectly by silencing TNF-α, which amplifies [[inflammasome]] activation upstream.⁴

---

## Novel therapeutic agents

Talabostat, an oral dipeptidyl peptidase inhibitor, triggers [[inflammasome]] priming and induces IL-1β and IL-18 secretion, bridging innate and adaptive immunity in small cell neuroendocrine prostate cancer.⁴ This approach exploits controlled IL-1β induction to alert and prime immune cells for enhanced checkpoint blockade efficacy.⁴ Conversely, salvianolic acid B formulated as cypate-modified organic quantum dots enables simultaneous IL-1β suppression and real-time near-infrared imaging of ischemic borders, integrating theranostic capabilities for intraoperative guidance.⁹ [[nmn|β-Nicotinamide mononucleotide]] represents a metabolic intervention that suppresses IL-1β through NAD⁺-dependent [[sirt1|SIRT1]] restoration, offering a strategy to improve the viability of marginal liver grafts by modulating macrophage phenotype.¹⁰
