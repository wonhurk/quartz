**G-CSF** (granulocyte-colony stimulating factor) is a pro-tumor inflammatory cytokine that drives immunosuppression within the tumor microenvironment by suppressing antitumor immune responses.¹ Its pathological upregulation in [[triple-negative-breast-cancer|triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC)]] is directly mediated by [[galectin-1|galectin-1 (GAL-1)]], a glyco-immune checkpoint protein that orchestrates a pro-tumor cytokine profile alongside [[vegf-a|VEGF-A]]. Selective inhibition of [[galectin-1|GAL-1]] with engineered minibodies effectively reduces G-CSF production, restoring antitumor immunity and substantially enhancing the efficacy of [[anti-pd-1-checkpoint|anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibitor]] therapy.¹

---

## Mechanism

G-CSF acts as a central node in the [[galectin-1|GAL-1]]-driven immunosuppressive program of TNBC cells. [[galectin-1|GAL-1]] directly induces G-CSF upregulation alongside [[vegf-a|VEGF-A]] expression, establishing a coordinated pro-tumor cytokine milieu that suppresses [[cd8-t-cells|CD8+ T cell]] activation and promotes regulatory T cell (Treg) differentiation.¹ This suppression of antitumor immune responses is mechanistically distinct from the immunomodulatory cytokine profile driven by [[galectin-7|GAL-7]], underscoring the non-redundant roles of different galectins in shaping tumor immunity.¹ The [[galectin-1|GAL-1]]-G-CSF axis enables immune evasion by dampening infiltration of activated effector T cells while promoting expansion of immunosuppressive populations within the tumor microenvironment.

---

## Triple-negative breast cancer

In TNBC, [[galectin-1|GAL-1]]-driven G-CSF production represents a major mechanism of immune escape and therapeutic resistance. The [[galectin-1|GAL-1]]-mediated upregulation of G-CSF suppresses antitumor immune responses, facilitating tumor progression and metastasis.¹ TNBC cells exploit this [[galectin-1|GAL-1]]-G-CSF signaling axis to create a permissive microenvironment hostile to T cell infiltration and activation, thereby evading both endogenous immune surveillance and exogenous checkpoint inhibitor-based immunotherapy.

---

## Therapeutic targeting and immunotherapy enhancement

Selective blockade of [[galectin-1|GAL-1]] with engineered nanobody-derived minibodies (G1M1) potently reduces G-CSF production and reverses TNBC immune evasion.¹ In vivo, [[galectin-1|GAL-1]] inhibition alone achieved tumor control and reduced lung metastases while increasing [[cd4-t-cells|CD4+ T cell]] infiltration and decreasing [[pd-1|PD-1+]] Treg populations, results comparable to anti-PD-1 monotherapy.¹ Critically, combining [[galectin-1|GAL-1]]-targeting minibodies with [[anti-pd-1-checkpoint|anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibitors]] produces synergistic antitumor immunity, demonstrating that G-CSF suppression via [[galectin-1|GAL-1]] inhibition is a clinically viable strategy to enhance checkpoint blockade efficacy in TNBC and potentially other immunotherapy-resistant cancers.¹
