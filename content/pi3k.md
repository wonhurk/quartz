**Phosphoinositide 3-kinase (PI3K)** is a family of lipid kinases that catalyzes the phosphorylation of phosphatidylinositol lipids at the 3-hydroxyl position of the inositol ring, generating lipid second messengers that coordinate cell survival, proliferation, migration, and metabolic homeostasis. The enzyme functions as a central signaling hub, transmitting extracellular growth factor and cytokine signals through the PI3K/[[akt|AKT]]/mTOR and PI3K/AKT/FoxO1 cascades to control protein synthesis, autophagy, glucose metabolism, and immune cell activation.¹ Aberrant PI3K hyperactivation is a hallmark of diverse malignancies including cervical, colorectal, lung squamous cell carcinoma, and follicular lymphoma, where it drives chemotherapy resistance, metastatic dissemination, and epithelial-mesenchymal plasticity.²³⁴ Beyond oncology, PI3K dysregulation underpins pathological inflammation, tissue fibrosis, metabolic disorders such as type 2 diabetes, and reproductive endocrine conditions including polycystic ovary syndrome and endometriosis.⁵⁶⁷

---

## Signaling architecture

Phosphoinositide 3-kinases are divided into three classes based on structural features and substrate specificity, with class I PI3Ks — comprising the catalytic isoforms PI3Kα, PI3Kβ, PI3Kγ, and PI3Kδ — being the most extensively characterized in disease pathogenesis. Upon receptor tyrosine kinase or G-protein-coupled receptor activation, class I PI3Ks convert phosphatidylinositol-4,5-bisphosphate (PIP₂) into phosphatidylinositol-3,4,5-trisphosphate (PIP₃), which recruits pleckstrin homology domain-containing effectors including [[akt|AKT]] and PDK1 to the plasma membrane.⁸ Activated AKT phosphorylates a vast network of substrates controlling cell cycle progression, apoptotic suppression via BAD and FoxO inactivation, and metabolic reprogramming through mTORC1 activation and glucose transporter translocation.¹ In hepatic tissue, PI3K integrates with the insulin receptor substrate 2 (IRS2) to orchestrate the PI3K-Akt/FoxO1 axis, which governs gluconeogenesis, glycogen synthesis, and lipid homeostasis — processes disrupted in insulin-resistant states such as type 2 diabetes.¹

## Cancer resistance and therapeutic targeting

In cervical cancer, persistent cisplatin exposure drives selection for cells with PI3K pathway hyperactivation mediated by upregulation of [[caveolin-1|caveolin-1 (CAV1)]], a membrane scaffolding protein that potentiates PI3K/AKT signaling and confers chemoresistance.² Genetic or pharmacological suppression of CAV1 restored cisplatin sensitivity in resistant cell lines, and [[simvastatin]] — a statin with pleiotropic anticancer properties — synergized with cisplatin by downregulating CAV1 and suppressing downstream PI3K/AKT phosphorylation, thereby reversing drug resistance and promoting apoptosis.² In colorectal cancer, the calcium-activated chloride channel [[ano1|ANO1 (TMEM16A)]] stabilizes a partial epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) phenotype by sustaining PI3K/AKT/mTOR and ERK signaling, enabling metastatic dissemination while preserving epithelial adhesion characteristics that facilitate colonization at distant sites.⁴ Knockdown of ANO1 dismantled the hybrid E-cadherin⁺/vimentin⁺ cellular architecture and selectively depleted the partial EMT subpopulation, suppressing invasion and inducing apoptosis.⁴

In lung squamous cell carcinoma, the PI3Kα-selective inhibitor [[cyh33|CYH33]] — currently in phase I/II clinical trials — potently attenuated metastatic progression through a dual mechanism targeting both intrinsic tumor cell motility and the tumor microenvironment.⁹ CYH33 disrupted cytoskeletal architecture and blocked PI3K signaling in cancer cells, while concurrently suppressing the trans-differentiation and proliferation of cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs), which otherwise secrete pro-migratory factors including hepatocyte growth factor that activate PI3K signaling in adjacent tumor cells.⁹ In orthotopic xenograft models, oral administration of CYH33 significantly reduced metastatic burden, with transcriptomic analysis revealing decreased CAF infiltration in primary tumors following treatment.⁹

For hematologic malignancies, the PI3Kδ-selective inhibitor [[linperlisib]] demonstrated clinical efficacy in patients with relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma who had received at least two prior lines of systemic therapy, leading to regulatory approval in China in November 2022 for third-line and beyond treatment.¹⁰ The isoform selectivity of linperlisib confers a distinct safety profile compared to pan-PI3K inhibitors, while retaining potent activity against malignant B cells dependent on PI3Kδ for survival signaling.¹⁰

## Immune regulation and inflammation

PI3K signaling serves as a critical node integrating danger signals and inflammatory responses in immune cells. In neutrophil-like cells differentiated from the HL-60 lineage, activation of the purinergic receptor [[p2x7r|P2X7R]] by extracellular ATP triggers PI3K/AKT-dependent stimulation of the ABC transporter [[mrp1|MRP1 (ABCC1)]], which exports glutathione (GSH) from the cytoplasm into the extracellular space.¹¹ This GSH efflux activates neutral sphingomyelinase (nSMase), catalyzing ceramide production and driving intraluminal vesicle formation within multivesicular endosomes — the biogenic pathway for exosome release.¹¹ Pharmacological inhibition of PI3K, AKT, or MRP1 prevented GSH efflux and abrogated exosome secretion, establishing PI3K as an essential signaling intermediate linking danger-associated molecular pattern recognition to intercellular communication via extracellular vesicles.¹¹

In tissue fibrosis, macrophage scavenger receptor 1 (MSR1) senses damage-associated molecular patterns at sites of injury, activating the MSR1/PI3K/AKT/[[spp1|SPP1 (osteopontin)]] signaling axis that drives macrophage hypermigration, activation, and pro-fibrotic programming.⁵ [[polyinosinic-acid|Polyinosinic acid (Poly I)]], a synthetic double-stranded RNA analog, competitively inhibits MSR1 signaling, thereby downregulating PI3K/AKT/SPP1 pathway activation and reducing macrophage recruitment to injured tissue.⁵ In a rat model of epidural fibrosis following laminectomy, local implantation of an electrospun polycaprolactone membrane incorporating Poly I for sustained release suppressed early macrophage aggregation and activation in the fibrotic zone, attenuating collagen deposition and adhesion formation over an eight-week observation period.⁵

## Metabolic and reproductive disease

In type 2 diabetes, hepatic insulin resistance disrupts the IRS2/PI3K-Akt/FoxO1 signaling pathway, impairing suppression of gluconeogenic gene expression and contributing to hyperglycemia. [[heterophyllin-b|Heterophyllin B (HB)]], a cyclic peptide derived from Pseudostellaria Radix, was systematically validated to alleviate diabetic pathology in high-fat diet/streptozotocin-induced mouse models by restoring IRS2/PI3K-Akt/FoxO1 signaling competence and normalizing bile acid and glycerophospholipid metabolism.¹ To overcome the poor oral bioavailability and P-glycoprotein efflux liability of HB, an engineered N-acetylgalactosamine-modified pillar[6]arene nanocarrier encapsulating HB and embedded within a mucoadhesive interpolymer complex micropatch achieved a 66.31% oral bioavailability — approximately 3.3-fold higher than free peptide — and outperformed metformin in glycemic control.¹

Across female reproductive disorders, PI3K-AKT dysregulation has emerged as a shared pathogenic mechanism linking polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL), and endometriosis.⁷ Network toxicology analysis integrating molecular docking and multi-omics data revealed that environmental exposures to phthalate esters (PAEs) and organophosphate esters (OPEs) — pervasive endocrine-disrupting plastic additives — converge on PI3K-AKT pathway perturbation alongside NF-κB activation and microRNA-mediated dysregulation.⁷ Molecular docking confirmed direct binding of DEHP, MEHP, and TPHP to disease-specific hub genes governing inflammatory signaling, suggesting that chronic low-dose chemical exposures exacerbate reproductive pathology through sustained PI3K pathway activation.⁷
