**Gold nanoparticles (AuNPs)** are versatile nanomaterials that have become essential components in advanced biomedical diagnostics and therapeutics, leveraging their unique optical, catalytic, and plasmonic properties for signal amplification and molecular recognition. These nanoscale gold constructs function as signal amplifiers and catalytic enhancers in lateral flow immunoassays, electrochemical sensors, and point-of-care diagnostic platforms across multiple clinical applications including cancer biomarker detection, viral antigen identification, and metabolic disorder screening.¹⁻⁹ Their surface plasmon resonance and metal-enhanced fluorescence effects substantially amplify analytical signals, reducing limits of detection and achieving signal enhancements up to 125-fold compared to conventional approaches.⁵

---

## Signal amplification mechanisms

AuNPs amplify electrochemical and optical signals through multiple synergistic mechanisms rooted in their nanoscale physical properties. The surface plasmon resonance effect generates localized electric fields under electrochemical or optical excitation, enhancing electron transfer kinetics and amplifying detection signals.² When integrated into [[electrochemiluminescence-biosensor\|electrochemiluminescence biosensors]], three-dimensional self-assembled AuNPs-ABEI nanoluminous spheres concentrate gold nanoparticles to accelerate interfacial electron transfer while immobilizing additional luminophore molecules, substantially improving luminescence intensity through dual-enhanced electron transfer mechanisms.² Metal-enhanced fluorescence provides an orthogonal amplification strategy, as demonstrated in calcium detection strips where AuNPs functionalized onto nitrocellulose membranes produce threefold fluorescence intensity increases compared to controls without gold enhancement.⁹ In electrochemical aptasensor platforms, AuNPs combined with three-dimensional reduced graphene oxide create high-surface-area conductive interfaces that enhance electron transfer, improve probe stability, and enable ultrasensitive detection with limits as low as 5 pg/mL.⁶

---

## Cancer biomarker detection

AuNPs enable ultrasensitive detection of multiple cancer biomarkers through diverse sensing architectures tailored to specific clinical needs. For prostate cancer diagnosis, multiplexed electrochemical aptasensors based on 3D-rGO/AuNP platforms detect urinary [[psma\|prostate-specific membrane antigen]] with 5 pg/mL sensitivity across a 15-2000 pg/mL linear range, offering a non-invasive alternative to blood-based PSA testing that suffers from poor specificity.⁶ Complementary approaches for prostate-specific antigen leverage AuNPs encapsulated with glucose oxidase within covalent organic frameworks, achieving near-zero background signal through dual catalytic amplification in chronocoulometric detection with 0.083 pg/mL limits and exceptional long-term stability.⁸ In colon cancer screening, DNA tetrahedral nanostructure probes combined with AuNPs-ABEI nanoluminous spheres detect methylated DNA patterns with unprecedented sensitivity by overcoming the poor binding efficiency and spatial steric hindrance characteristic of complex clinical samples.² Hierarchically mesoporous channel-confined nanozyme reactors ([[fe-mpp-t\|FeMPPt]]) enrich and catalyze gold plasmonic signal amplification in visual lateral flow immunoassays, achieving 125-fold and 1300-fold sensitivity improvements over conventional gold nanoparticle labels for cardiac troponin I and alpha-fetoprotein respectively, with clinical validation across 268 serum samples demonstrating performance comparable or superior to standard laboratory methods.⁵

## Viral antigen detection

AuNPs serve as platforms for engineering abiotic antibody mimics that target viral antigens with specificity and neutralization capacity comparable to natural antibodies. Gold nanoparticle cores functionalized with rationally designed hydrogel polymer nanoparticles ([[atrp-np7\|Au@ATrp-NP7]]) bind [[sars-cov-2\|SARS-CoV-2]] spike receptor-binding domain through synergistic steric effects arising from the chemical identity and spatial arrangement of anionic carboxyl and aromatic indolyl groups.⁷ The key epitopes and residues involved in ATrp-NP7 binding coincide with ACE2-binding sites, enabling competitive inhibition of spike RBD-ACE2 interactions with efficacy matching antibodies while offering mutation tolerance and biomolecule-free lateral flow immunoassay capabilities for point-of-care testing of spike antigens and pseudoviruses.⁷

## Hybrid nanozyme architectures

When incorporated into hybrid nanozyme architectures, AuNPs enhance catalytic activity and electron transfer while enabling oriented antibody immobilization strategies that preserve both antigen-binding reactivity and nanozyme functionality. [[au-pt-nanozymes\|Au@Pt core-shell nanoparticles]] conjugated to antibodies via aptamer-mediated oriented immobilization ensure that antigen-binding domains remain fully accessible while preserving peroxidase-mimicking activity, yielding 13-fold detection limit reductions compared to conventional gold nanoparticle immunoassays in lateral flow platforms for anti-Müllerian hormone.¹ Prussian blue nanocubes decorated with molybdenum disulfide shells and functionalized with AuNPs (PB@MoS2@Au) integrate enhanced peroxidase-like activity with 37.4% photothermal conversion efficiency within the mild-hyperthermia window, enabling combined catalytic and thermal antibacterial treatment.⁴ The hierarchical mesoporosity of FeMPPt nanozyme reactors confines platinum nanozymes within dendritic mesoporous silica channels, with electrostatic adsorption and mesoporous confinement catalysis producing high colorimetric signal brightness for dual-mode qualitative and quantitative detection of multiplex disease biomarkers.⁵

## Photothermal immunotherapy

Gold nanorods integrated into thermoresponsive chitosan-based composite hydrogels enable sustained tumor immunotherapy through sequential release mechanisms and synergistic photothermal effects. Indocyanine green-loaded gold nanorods within boronic acid-modified mesoporous silica are embedded in carboxymethyl chitosan matrices that undergo in situ gelation at physiological temperature, forming durable depots for sustained and tumor microenvironment-responsive release.³ Gradual leaching of β-glycerophosphate combined with enzymatic degradation of chitosan sustains the release of ICG-loaded gold nanorods, while cleavage of boronate esters in the acidic tumor microenvironment triggers the release of both indocyanine green and gold-silica particles for synergistic photothermal and reactive oxygen species-mediated tumor cell apoptosis.³ Concurrently, chitosan modulates immune feedback to amplify immune activation and infiltration, while the photothermal effects reprogram tumor-associated macrophages toward an anti-tumor phenotype, markedly suppressing tumor growth and metastasis.³

## Non-cancer applications

Beyond oncology diagnostics and immunotherapy, AuNPs address critical needs in oral infectious disease treatment and veterinary metabolic disorder screening. The PB@MoS2@Au hybrid nanozyme combined with low-concentration hydrogen peroxide and near-infrared irradiation exhibits potent antibacterial efficacy against seven representative oral bacterial strains in dental caries, endodontic infections, and periodontitis, achieving bactericidal rates as high as 95% and eradicating over 99.5% of Enterococcus faecalis biofilms from root canals within minutes in ex vivo tooth infection models.⁴ In dairy cattle health management, gold nanoparticle-functionalized test strips quantify ionized calcium in bovine plasma and milk for rapid detection of hypocalcemia, a severe metabolic disease affecting up to 50% of multiparous cows postpartum.⁹ The metal-enhanced fluorescence effect amplifies signals from the calcium-sensitive dye Fluo-5 N, enabling simple dip-and-read operation with 30-second readout times, strong linear correlations across clinically relevant concentrations, and an estimated production cost of $0.14 CAD per strip.⁹

---

## Point-of-care platform integration

AuNP-based lateral flow immunoassays provide rapid, user-friendly diagnostic capabilities with minimal training requirements and cost-effective manufacturing. The hierarchically mesoporous nanozyme reactor approach yields qualitative and quantitative detection platforms validated in clinical emergency departments with readout times under one minute and per-test costs orders of magnitude lower than centralized laboratory instrumentation.⁵ Entirely biomolecule-free lateral flow immunoassays constructed by integrating gold-core polymer nanoparticle detection probes, polymer nanoparticle capture units, and synthetic antibody mimics eliminate cold-chain storage requirements and batch-to-batch antibody variability while maintaining diagnostic performance comparable to conventional immunoassays.⁷ The multiplexed three-working-electrode screen-printed platform configuration enables simultaneous triplicate measurements from single samples, improving analytical precision and reducing variability in continuous biomarker monitoring applications.⁶
