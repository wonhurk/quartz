**Ferroptosis** is an iron-dependent form of regulated cell death mechanistically distinct from [[apoptosis]], characterized by the accumulation of lipid peroxides and the depletion of glutathione (GSH). Its central molecular brake is [[gpx4|glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4)]], whose inactivation unleashes uncontrolled lipid peroxidation leading to membrane rupture and cell death.¹ The pathway is tightly regulated by the cystine/glutamate antiporter [[slc7a11|xCT (SLC7A11)]], iron metabolism enzymes including [[tfrc|TFRC]], and [[nox4]]-driven reactive oxygen species generation.

---

## Mechanism

Ferroptosis is initiated when intracellular iron catalyzes the Fenton reaction, producing hydroxyl radicals that oxidize polyunsaturated fatty acids in cell membranes. [[acsl4|ACSL4]] plays an essential role by activating long-chain fatty acids for incorporation into phospholipids, making cells vulnerable to peroxidation.² The system [[slc7a11|xCT/SLC7A11]] imports cystine for GSH synthesis, and its inhibition — by erastin or [[sorafenib]] — depletes the cellular antioxidant pool and sensitizes cells to ferroptosis.³ Once GSH is exhausted, [[gpx4|GPX4]] can no longer detoxify lipid hydroperoxides, and cell death proceeds irreversibly.

## Cancer applications

Ferroptosis has emerged as a powerful therapeutic strategy across multiple malignancies, particularly where conventional chemotherapy fails. In [[triple-negative-breast-cancer|triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC)]] — a subtype defined by absence of druggable hormone receptors — ferroptosis-based nanoprodrug systems and [[jph203]]-loaded nanoassemblies have demonstrated synergistic cytotoxicity.⁴ In [[melanoma]], the natural alkaloid [[neferine]] simultaneously induces ferroptosis and suppresses [[pd-l1|PD-L1]] expression, reversing immune evasion.¹

Resistance to ferroptosis is an emerging clinical challenge. In castration-resistant prostate cancer, loss of [[decorin]] suppresses [[acsl4|ACSL4]]-dependent lipid remodeling, driving resistance to [[enzalutamide]].² Similarly, [[usp20|USP20]] deubiquitinates and stabilizes GPX4, enabling ferroptosis evasion and tyrosine kinase inhibitor resistance across several cancer types.⁵ In ovarian cancer, [[zdhhc14|ZDHHC14]]-driven S-palmitoylation of RUNX2 attenuates ferroptosis via the YAP1/GLS1 axis.⁶

## Immunotherapy synergy

Ferroptosis induction triggers immunogenic cell death, releasing damage-associated molecular patterns that activate dendritic cells and promote [[cd8-t-cells|CD8+ T cell]] infiltration. Ferroptotic tumor cells also reprogram tumor-associated macrophage antigen presentation, enhancing the efficacy of immune checkpoint blockade.⁷ The [[sting|STING]] pathway is activated during ferroptosis, driving type I interferon production and establishing durable immunological memory.⁸ Concurrently, ferroptosis paradoxically upregulates [[pd-l1|PD-L1]], which can dampen the immune response — a mechanism exploitable by combining ferroptosis inducers with [[anti-pd-l1-antibody|anti-PD-L1 antibodies]].

---

## Non-cancer diseases

Beyond oncology, ferroptosis plays pathological roles in several organ systems. In the central nervous system, aberrant ferroptosis — mediated by [[nox4|NOX4]] upregulation via the miR-10b-3p axis — contributes to neuronal loss in [[alzheimers-disease|Alzheimer's disease]] models.⁹ In the heart, IGF-1 protects against [[obesity-cardiomyopathy|obesity-induced cardiomyopathy]] by suppressing ferroptosis through arachidylcarnitine-dependent mechanisms.¹⁰ In [[age-related-macular-degeneration|age-related macular degeneration]], iron homeostasis disruption drives retinal cell ferroptosis, addressable by triple-pool-targeted nanotherapy.¹¹

---

## Novel inducers

[[resveratrol|Resveratrol]] was identified through multi-omics analysis as a ferroptosis inducer in [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma]] by targeting the core ferroptosis gene signature.¹² [[neferine|Neferine]] acts through a metal-organic framework delivery system, simultaneously inducing ferroptosis and suppressing immune evasion.¹ [[thioredoxin-reductase|Thioredoxin reductase]] inhibitors and Pt(IV) prodrugs further expand the ferroptosis-inducing arsenal by targeting redox homeostasis.
