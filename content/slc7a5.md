**SLC7A5** (Solute Carrier Family 7 Member 5) is an amino acid transporter that functions as a critical metabolic checkpoint and ferroptosis suppressor in cancer cells, mediating leucine and [[methionine]] uptake to sustain tumor cell growth and survival. The protein's primary mechanism involves activation of [[mtorc1|mTORC1]] signaling downstream of leucine import, while simultaneously suppressing the iron-dependent regulated cell death pathway [[ferroptosis|ferroptosis]].¹ In [[glioblastoma]], [[yap|YAP]]-mediated transcriptional upregulation of SLC7A5 drives [[methionine]] addiction and radioresistance through a [[prmt5|PRMT5]]-dependent feedback loop.² Pharmacological inhibition of SLC7A5 with [[jph203|JPH203]] effectively triggers ferroptosis in [[triple-negative-breast-cancer|triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC)]] by disrupting iron and redox homeostasis, achieving an 81.2% tumor suppression rate while simultaneously activating dendritic cells and CD8+ T-cell immunity.¹

---

## Mechanism

SLC7A5 functions as the light chain of the system L amino acid transporter, mediating the uptake of large neutral amino acids—particularly leucine and [[methionine]]—in exchange for cytoplasmic glutamine. Following leucine import, intracellular leucine accumulation activates [[mtorc1|mTORC1]], a central metabolic hub that drives protein synthesis, lipid biogenesis, and cellular proliferation.¹ Beyond its role as a leucine transporter, SLC7A5 operates as a critical node in the ferroptosis-suppressive network. Ferroptosis suppression by SLC7A5 appears mechanistically linked to its role in amino acid homeostasis: leucine uptake and [[mtorc1|mTORC1]] activation suppress ferroptotic cell death through enhanced amino acid-dependent biosynthetic and redox repair pathways.¹ When SLC7A5 is pharmacologically inhibited, intracellular amino acid pools contract, iron and redox homeostasis become destabilized, and cells become acutely vulnerable to lipid peroxidation-driven ferroptotic collapse.

## Triple-Negative Breast Cancer Therapy

TNBC represents one of the most aggressive breast cancer subtypes, characterized by the absence of hormone receptors and HER2, leaving conventional endocrine and HER2-targeted therapies ineffective. SLC7A5 has been identified as a ferroptosis-suppressing prognostic target in TNBC, elevated in tumors with poor clinical outcomes. Pharmacological targeting via [[jph203|JPH203]], a selective SLC7A5 inhibitor, effectively triggers ferroptotic cell death in TNBC by blocking leucine uptake, suppressing [[mtorc1|mTORC1]] signaling, and disrupting iron and redox homeostasis.¹ In orthotopic TNBC models, a tumor microenvironment-responsive [[jph203|JPH203]]-delivery nanoassembly achieved an 81.2% tumor growth suppression rate with favorable tolerability.¹

Beyond direct tumor killing, [[jph203|JPH203]]-induced ferroptosis simultaneously engages innate and adaptive immunity. Ferroptotic TNBC cells release damage-associated molecular patterns that promote [[dendritic-cell|dendritic cell]] maturation and antigen presentation, driving robust [[cd8-t-cell|CD8+ T-cell]] infiltration and activation within the tumor microenvironment.¹ This dual mechanism—simultaneous ferroptotic cell death and immune activation—results in significantly improved survival outcomes, establishing SLC7A5 inhibition as a promising synthetic lethal strategy in TNBC.

## Glioblastoma and Radioresistance

In [[glioblastoma]], SLC7A5 is transcriptionally upregulated by [[yap|YAP]] within a [[prmt5|PRMT5]]-dependent signaling axis that confers both tumorigenicity and radioresistance. The mechanism operates as follows: [[methionine]] availability activates [[prmt5|PRMT5]]-mediated symmetrical dimethylation of [[yap|YAP]] at arginine 124 (R124me2s), which protects [[yap|YAP]] from inhibitory phosphorylation and facilitates its nuclear translocation and transcriptional activation.² This activated [[yap|YAP]] then transcriptionally upregulates both [[prmt5|PRMT5]] itself and the [[methionine]] transporters [[slc3a2|SLC3A2]] and SLC7A5, establishing a feedforward loop that drives [[methionine]] addiction and sustained [[yap|YAP]] hyperactivation.² Critically, ionizing radiation activates this entire [[prmt5|PRMT5]]-[[yap|YAP]]-SLC7A5 axis, enabling glioblastoma cells to survive radiotherapy through heightened amino acid import and [[mtorc1|mTORC1]]-driven survival signaling.²

Blocking the [[prmt5|PRMT5]]-[[yap|YAP]]-SLC7A5 axis in combination with radiotherapy significantly impairs intracranial glioblastoma growth and extends survival in treated animals.² This dual-axis targeting exploits glioblastoma's metabolic vulnerability to [[methionine]] and demonstrates that disrupting SLC7A5-dependent amino acid import can overcome radioresistance, positioning SLC7A5 as a critical therapeutic target for overcoming treatment-resistant disease.

---

## Metabolic Partnerships

SLC7A5 functions as the light chain partner of [[slc3a2|SLC3A2]], the heavy chain of the system L transporter complex in cancer cells.² While [[slc3a2|SLC3A2]] provides the structural and trafficking scaffold, SLC7A5 confers leucine and [[methionine]] selectivity and transport capacity. In [[glioblastoma]], both transporter subunits are co-upregulated by [[yap|YAP]]-mediated transcription, enabling coordinated expansion of the [[methionine]] import capacity and driving the feedforward amplification of [[prmt5|PRMT5]]-[[yap|YAP]] signaling.² This metabolic partnership underscores why combined targeting of both transporter subunits may be necessary to fully disrupt [[methionine]] addiction in certain tumor contexts.
