**Transforming Growth Factor-Beta (TGF-β)** is a multifunctional cytokine superfamily that orchestrates fibrotic tissue remodeling and immunosuppressive signaling across diverse pathological contexts. The canonical TGF-β/[[smad3|SMAD3]] pathway serves as the principal mechanism by which TGF-β drives pathological fibrosis in lungs, kidneys, endometrium, and diabetic complications.²⁴⁶⁷ TGF-β1, the predominant isoform, mediates both profibrotic effects in chronic tissue injury and immunosuppressive functions within tumor microenvironments, promoting regulatory T cell differentiation and M2 macrophage polarization.¹⁵⁸ These dual activities position TGF-β as a central node linking tissue remodeling to immune evasion in cancer progression.

---

## Mechanism

TGF-β signals through transmembrane serine/threonine kinase receptors (TGFβRI and TGFβRII) that phosphorylate [[smad3|SMAD3]], which translocates to the nucleus and activates transcription of profibrotic genes encoding extracellular matrix components, matrix metalloproteinase inhibitors, and inflammatory mediators.²⁴ In gastric cancer, tumor cell-derived serglycin binds [[cd44|CD44]] to trigger the CD44-TGFβRI-SMAD3 cascade, sustaining [[lag3|LAG3]]-positive regulatory T cell differentiation and suppressive function.¹ TGF-β1 also upregulates collagen deposition, suppresses matrix degradation, and perpetuates chronic inflammation by recruiting M2-polarized macrophages that secrete additional TGF-β1, creating a feed-forward fibrotic loop.⁶⁸

---

## Fibrotic diseases

In [[idiopathic-pulmonary-fibrosis|idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF)]], TGF-β1 drives aberrant myofibroblast activation and excessive collagen accumulation, resulting in progressive lung stiffening and respiratory failure.⁷⁸ [[pirfenidone|Pirfenidone]], a clinically approved antifibrotic agent, inhibits TGF-β1-induced profibrotic marker expression in lung fibroblasts, and [[pfd-loaded-hsa-nanoparticles|pirfenidone-loaded human serum albumin nanoparticles]] optimized for inhalation delivery achieve superior lung retention and antifibrotic efficacy while minimizing systemic toxicity.⁷

In [[diabetic-nephropathy|diabetic kidney disease (DKD)]], dysregulated iron metabolism in proximal tubules correlates with elevated TGF-β signaling and [[renal-fibrosis|renal fibrosis]].⁴ The iron ionophore [[beta-thujaplicin|β-Thujaplicin]] mobilizes tubular iron deposits, suppresses TGF-β pathway activation, reduces oxidative stress, and attenuates fibrotic marker expression in high-glucose and TGF-β1-stimulated renal tubular cells.⁴ Similarly, oral DHA-acylated astaxanthin ester exerts renoprotective effects in diabetic mice by remodeling gut microbiota, increasing colonic trehalose production, and promoting renal [[hsp90aa1|HSP90AA1]]-mediated stress resistance, thereby suppressing TGF-β-driven renal inflammation and fibrosis.⁶

[[intrauterine-adhesions|Intrauterine adhesions (IUA)]] result from abnormal TGF-β/SMAD3 activation following endometrial injury, leading to endometrial fibrosis and infertility.² An injectable hydrogel delivering [[smad3|SMAD3]]-targeting siRNA achieves sustained gene silencing, exhibits antibacterial activity, promotes endometrial angiogenesis and cellular proliferation, and restores fertility in preclinical models.²

## Cancer immunosuppression

TGF-β1 is a potent immunosuppressive cytokine that facilitates tumor immune evasion through multiple mechanisms. In gastric cancer, TGF-β promotes the differentiation of [[lag3|LAG3]]-positive [[regulatory-t-cells|regulatory T cells]] by activating the CD44-TGFβRI-SMAD3 pathway, which triggers glycolysis, clears reactive oxygen species, reduces oxidative modification of LAG3 at methionine 171, enhances LAG3 dimerization, and stabilizes LAG3 by preventing K48-linked ubiquitination.¹ This results in an expanded population of highly suppressive Tregs that inhibit antitumor immunity.

In [[hepatocellular-carcinoma|hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)]], TGF-β signaling drives angiogenesis via VEGF pathway activation and promotes M0 macrophage polarization into tumor-associated macrophages.⁵ The ubiquitination-associated gene [[kcmf1|KCMF1]] is overexpressed in HCC and positively correlates with TGF-β pathway enrichment and immunosuppressive M0 macrophage infiltration.⁵ Spatial transcriptomics reveals that [[ube2c|UBE2C]]-high regions within HCC exhibit TGFB1 enrichment, impaired CXCL9-CXCR3 chemokine signaling, cytotoxic T cell exclusion, and resistance to immunotherapy.¹⁰

[[m2-macrophages|M2 macrophages]], whose polarization is driven by TGF-β and [[adora2b|ADORA2B]] signaling, secrete elevated levels of TGF-β1 and other profibrotic mediators, amplifying both fibrosis and immunosuppression within the tumor microenvironment.⁸

---

## Ferroptosis pathway

TGF-β1 has been identified as a core gene within the [[ferroptosis]] regulatory network in [[pancreatic-ductal-adenocarcinoma|pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC)]].⁹ Integrative multi-omics analysis combining transcriptomics, machine learning, and single-cell RNA sequencing identified six key ferroptosis-related genes—[[gpx4|GPX4]], CTSB, NOX4, TFRC, HIF1A, and TGFB1—that are enriched in ferroptosis, HIF-1, and TGF-β pathways and correlate with M1/M2 macrophage infiltration.⁹ High TGFB1 expression predicts poor prognosis in PDAC. [[resveratrol|Resveratrol]], validated through molecular docking and in vitro assays, downregulates TGFB1 and multiple ferroptosis-related proteins, suppresses tumor cell viability, and enhances ferroptosis sensitivity, effects that are partially reversed by the ferroptosis inhibitor ferrostatin-1.⁹

---

## Therapeutic strategies

Multiple therapeutic strategies converge on suppressing TGF-β/SMAD3 signaling. Gene silencing via [[smad3|SMAD3]]-targeting siRNA delivered through injectable hydrogels achieves sustained antifibrotic effects in endometrial fibrosis.² [[pirfenidone|Pirfenidone]], formulated as nebulizable nanoparticles, inhibits TGF-β1-induced profibrotic responses in lung fibroblasts and maintains stability post-nebulization, enabling efficient pulmonary delivery.⁷ [[beta-thujaplicin|β-Thujaplicin]] mobilizes renal tubular iron and inhibits TGF-β signaling, reducing oxidative stress and fibrosis in diabetic kidney disease.⁴

In pulmonary fibrosis, biomimetic nanoparticles coated with M2 macrophage membranes codeliver mulberrin and [[adora2b|ADORA2B]]-targeting siRNA, activating the [[nrf2|Nrf2]] antioxidant pathway, suppressing reactive oxygen species production, silencing ADORA2B expression, reducing M2 macrophage infiltration, and diminishing downstream TGF-β secretion.⁸ [[photobiomodulation-therapy|Photobiomodulation therapy]] combined with [[decellularized-dermal-scaffold|decellularized dermal scaffolds]] modulates inflammatory and matrix remodeling responses in diabetic wounds, improving tissue integrity and reducing profibrotic signaling.³
