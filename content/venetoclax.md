**Venetoclax** is a selective B-cell lymphoma 2 (BCL-2) inhibitor that promotes apoptosis in lymphoid malignancies, fundamentally reshaping treatment paradigms for [[chronic-lymphocytic-leukemia|chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL)]] and [[hodgkin-lymphoma|Hodgkin lymphoma]].¹ By directly antagonizing [[bcl2|BCL-2]] — the cardinal anti-apoptotic protein conferring survival to hematopoietic malignancies — venetoclax overcomes apoptosis resistance and has become the cornerstone of chemoimmunotherapy-free regimens.¹ Deployed as part of combination therapy with [[btk|Bruton tyrosine kinase (BTK)]] inhibitors such as [[acalabrutinib]], venetoclax has emerged as a transformative alternative to conventional chemotherapy, delivering durable disease control in treatment-naive patients.²

---

## Mechanism

Venetoclax exerts its cytotoxic effect through selective, high-affinity binding to the BH3-binding groove of [[bcl2|BCL-2]], displacing pro-apoptotic family members including BAX and BAK from sequestration at the mitochondrial outer membrane. This direct antagonism unleashes the intrinsic apoptotic cascade, triggering caspase activation and programmed cell death. In primary Hodgkin Reed-Sternberg (HRS) cells isolated from pediatric [[hodgkin-lymphoma|Hodgkin lymphoma]] lesions, venetoclax augments apoptosis induction despite the presence of disordered cellular differentiation and elevated senescence markers, indicating that BCL-2 inhibition can pierce multiple layers of apoptosis resistance.² The drug's selectivity for BCL-2 — as opposed to related anti-apoptotic proteins like BCL-XL — renders it particularly potent in malignancies where BCL-2 dependency is high, such as CLL and HRS cells.

## Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

Venetoclax has revolutionized CLL treatment by enabling effective chemoimmunotherapy-free combinations, particularly when paired with [[btk|BTK]] inhibitors. In treatment-naive patients without del(17p) or TP53 mutations, the combination of venetoclax with the BTK inhibitor [[acalabrutinib]], with or without the anti-CD20 antibody [[obinutuzumab]], achieves remarkable disease control. The phase 3 AMPLIFY study demonstrated that acalabrutinib-venetoclax (with or without obinutuzumab) achieves a 3-year progression-free survival of 78.9%, substantially exceeding outcomes with conventional chemoimmunotherapy regimens including fludarabine-cyclophosphamide-rituximab or bendamustine-rituximab.¹ This outcome represents a pivotal departure from intensive chemotherapy in the frontline CLL treatment landscape. The synergistic efficacy of BTK inhibition and BCL-2 antagonism reflects complementary mechanisms: BTK pathway inhibition disrupts B-cell receptor survival signaling, while venetoclax directly primes the apoptotic machinery by targeting the final anti-apoptotic checkpoint. By eliminating dependence on chemotherapy, this regimen reduces cumulative toxicity and enables sustained quality of life.

## Hodgkin lymphoma

In pediatric [[hodgkin-lymphoma|Hodgkin lymphoma]], venetoclax offers a therapeutic strategy for targeting apoptosis resistance in the rare malignant HRS cells that orchestrate disease pathogenesis. Primary HRS cells isolated from pediatric HL lesions display a distinctive transcriptional landscape characterized by disordered differentiation with multilineage gene expression patterns, coupled with upregulation of senescence-related genes and paradoxical downregulation of pro-apoptotic machinery. In vitro exposure of primary HRS cells to venetoclax augmented apoptosis induction, demonstrating that [[bcl2|BCL-2]] inhibition can overcome the apoptosis-resistant state even in the context of cellular maturation disorder.² These findings support further investigation of BCL-2 inhibition as a means to dismantle the inflammatory lesions characteristic of pediatric HL and eliminate the sparse but pathogenic malignant cell population driving disease progression.
