**Depression** is a complex mood disorder characterized by pathological dysregulations affecting brain and body systems, including impaired hippocampal neurogenesis, altered plasma protein signatures, and metabolic dysfunction.¹ The disorder manifests through [[chronic-stress|chronic stress]]-mediated upregulation of corticotropin-releasing hormone receptors in the hippocampal dentate gyrus, where neurogenic capacity is selectively compromised in susceptible individuals.² Sex differences in stress susceptibility are prominent—females exhibit greater vulnerability to stress-induced depression with anhedonia and impaired early adult neurogenesis, while males demonstrate resilience despite similar elevation of stress hormone receptors.²

---

## Mechanism

Depression arises from the convergence of neurobiological, metabolic, and endocrine pathways. [[Chronic-stress|Chronic stress]] triggers upregulation of corticotropin-releasing hormone receptors throughout the hippocampal dentate gyrus in both sexes, yet only susceptible females develop impaired early adult neurogenesis markers and reduced dendritic spine density in this critical neurogenic niche.² Proteomic analysis of sex-specific responses reveals differential regulation of [[14-3-3-proteins|14-3-3 family proteins]], alongside disruptions in cell cycle regulation, Hippo signaling, PI3K/AKT signaling, and axon guidance pathways.² The brain-body axis is further implicated through multimodal fusion of brain imaging and proteomics, which demonstrates that plasma protein signatures of depression interact bidirectionally with structural and functional brain alterations, linking mood dysregulation to systemic metabolic dysfunction.¹

## Cancer-related depression

Patients with [[oral-cavity-cancer|oral cavity cancer]] experience heightened [[psychological-distress|psychological distress]] driven by visible disfigurement, speech and swallowing impairments, and associated social stigma, which contribute to elevated anxiety, depression, and social withdrawal.⁵ Pharmacological management in this population has been evaluated through prospective studies comparing mirtazapine—a noradrenergic and specific serotonergic antidepressant—and olanzapine, an atypical antipsychotic, both of which demonstrate efficacy in managing cancer-related distress while preserving acceptable safety profiles.⁵

---

## Dermatological comorbidity

Atopic dermatitis, a chronic immune-mediated inflammatory disease, is characterized by intense itch, eczematous rash, and skin pain that impose substantial burdens on quality of life, sleep, and mental health.³ Patients with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis exhibit disproportionately high rates of anxiety and depression, driven by the visible and symptomatic nature of the disease.³ Long-term treatment with upadacitinib, a selective Janus kinase inhibitor, has demonstrated sustained improvements in patient-reported outcomes over 140 weeks, including deep responses in mental health domains, underscoring the critical role of addressing inflammatory drivers in comorbid psychiatric symptoms.³

---

## Caregiver burden

Informal caregivers of individuals with dementia—predominantly family members—exhibit disproportionately high rates of [[psychological-distress|psychological distress]], depressive symptoms, and perceived burden, driven by the relentless demands of daily care provision.⁶ Traditional [[mindfulness-based-intervention|mindfulness-based interventions]] have demonstrated moderate-to-large effect sizes for improving caregiver mental health, yet face-to-face sessions and audio-guided home practice are constrained by low adherence, scheduling conflicts, and limited ecological validity.⁶ Immersive virtual reality technology offers a novel platform to simulate restorative natural environments, minimize external distractions, and standardize mindfulness instructions while preserving temporal flexibility, with emerging evidence suggesting enhanced engagement and affective outcomes compared to audio-only formats.⁶

---

## Therapeutic approaches

[[Aurantii-fructus|Aurantii Fructus]], a traditional Chinese medicine historically used to regulate Qi and alleviate emotional distress, exerts rapid antidepressant effects mediated by the hypoxanthine-caspase-4 axis in chronic unpredictable mild stress models, validating its traditional psychotropic indication.⁴ Pharmacological strategies span noradrenergic antidepressants (mirtazapine), atypical antipsychotics (olanzapine), and immunomodulatory agents including selective Janus kinase inhibitors that address both inflammatory and psychiatric pathology in comorbid conditions.³⁵ [[Mindfulness-based-intervention|Mindfulness-based interventions]], particularly those delivered through immersive virtual reality platforms, represent an evidence-based non-pharmacological modality with potential for scalable implementation in home environments.⁶

---

## Digital monitoring

Longitudinal [[mobile-health|mobile health]] measures combined with functional data analysis and machine learning algorithms enable prediction of individual depression trajectories in major depressive disorder, addressing the critical clinical challenge of treatment optimization and prognosis.⁷ Continuous follow-up facilitated by mHealth platforms captures temporal dynamics of symptom variation, overcoming limitations of static assessments and poor patient adherence through adaptive modeling approaches.⁷ These predictive frameworks hold promise for precision psychiatry, enabling early identification of relapse risk and dynamic adjustment of therapeutic strategies based on individualized trajectory forecasts.⁷
